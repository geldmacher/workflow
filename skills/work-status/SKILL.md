# work-status

Read the [working agreement](../../references/workflow.md). Stay read-only. From the available plan, assignments, and reports, explain what is complete and what remains uncertain. Identify the supporting documents and working state. Do not manufacture status from an identifier or treat absent reports as completion. Distinguish a completed phase from an achieved goal. If work remains, give the next useful action, who takes it, and why; ask for missing context precisely. Follow the documented phase outcome without starting another phase. If the goal is established as achieved, state that no further Workflow step is required.
