# Continuity engineering playbooks

## `session-pickup`

Read the approved plan, current assignment, relevant implementation and review reports, repository state, open decisions, and next action. Use accessible task references or complete supplied text. Compare inherited observations with the current repository; only affected claims need rechecking. Do not redo established work to recreate a formal trail. An agent's assertion of approval does not replace the human instruction.

## `pause-safely`

Stop at a resumable boundary, preserving incomplete and unrelated work. Provide the approved plan and current assignment, useful reports, actual working state, completed outcomes, limitations, and the first resume action in the native task. Name any outstanding decision. Do not commit, publish, change goals, or persist learning just to make the pause durable.
