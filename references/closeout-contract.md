# Review-owned Evidence

Cursor and Codex Manual do not close out implementation. Native **Implement Plan** and authorized corrections finish normally; no typed attestation, host Stop continuation, baseline gate, delivery report, or recovery command is part of their implementation lifecycle. Cursor does use a passive, `failClosed:false` planning `stop` observer to bind one exact current Root when native CreatePlan hooks are omitted. It always returns `{}`, emits no `followup_message`, and never creates another turn. Cursor may issue a short-lived Review-call receipt later, only inside an explicit Review turn.

Fresh same-task Review is the Manual trust boundary. In Cursor, the reviewer supplies fresh Check observations and closed review input to `workflow_closeout` with `artifact_kind: work-review`; a protected five-minute single-use host receipt supplies the exact approved native Schema-5 Root and validated current-task predecessors. In Codex and portable clients the reviewer supplies those exact bytes directly. The server observes the repository, derives full or delta Evidence when required, then creates Evidence and Review atomically. Missing predecessor bytes cause an honest full rebuild. Both artifacts are returned or neither is emitted.

Caller-supplied paths and snapshots are non-authoritative. The complete observable dirty inventory is considered; ambiguity or out-of-authority work becomes a limitation or Finding. A known failed required Check yields a completed blocked Review. Manual `verified` requires a fresh direct reviewer observation; host receipts are optional enrichment. High risk and Hard Triggers require the full auditor route.

The delivery-evidence mode of `workflow_closeout`, legacy attestation parsing, and content-addressed handoff remain wire-compatible for portable clients. They do not grant Cursor or Codex Manual task authority. Supervised and Autonomous certification remains unchanged.
