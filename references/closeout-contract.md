# Closeout contract

Fresh same-task Review is the Manual trust boundary. Cursor supplies a protected single-use receipt bound to the exact Schema-6 Root, canonical workspace, current selection, and predecessor bytes. Codex and portable clients supply exact bytes directly.

The builder preserves an active valid Root across recoverable transport failures. Missing selection is reported separately from missing Root. Presentation defects or absent harness evidence create limitations; they do not invent Root-unavailable.

The project harness returns a generic PhaseResult with snapshots, changed paths, Check attestations, usage, and limitations. Workflow creates Delivery Evidence and Work Review together or neither. A known failed required Check blocks delivery. Missing attestation remains provisional. Closeout never deploys, publishes, pushes, merges, or learns.
