# Adaptive design contract

Workflow 5 does not encode design quality as eight mandatory tables or a fixed depth ladder. The Intent Root states observable goal, acceptance, boundaries, constraints, non-goals, and risk. The Planner may use prose, lists, or tables.

The Strategy selects a versioned task recipe and small executable steps. It may revise decomposition, adjacent in-root targets, tools, and equivalent checks without a gate. A change to Intent, public contract, risk, dependency authority, external effects, or approval-required paths needs a human boundary decision.
