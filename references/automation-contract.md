# Adaptive authorization contract

Human decision fixes immutable Schema-5 Intent Root: goal, acceptance, constraints, public contract, risk, dependencies, effects, delivery boundary, budgets, protected paths.

Hash-chained `execution-strategy` may revise targets, steps, equivalent Checks, tools, approved models inside `authority.allowed_roots`; deviations enter the Ledger. One Writer owns the worktree; two readers max write external proof only.

`supervised` permits Dirty Baseline/Strategy adaptation but needs human acceptance. `autonomous` needs exact Qualification Key: task class, Verification Profile/Route Pool hashes, certified region. Non-safety deficit downgrades to `supervised`; authority/secret/budget violations stop.

Delivery is outer effect. Reviewers propose Learning; separate human `/learn-from-work` applies it after integration. Push/PR/merge/deploy/production/auto-integration/learning are forbidden.

Journey stays task-local; Runs are resilient, not a chat gate. Blockers state one reason/recovery.
