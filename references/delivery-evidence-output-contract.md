Return the generated artifact byte-for-byte. A cache-only or roots-discovery-only failure leaves an explicitly rooted artifact valid and requires the returned attach instruction. The result reports `workspace_binding`, `workspace_root_used`, `handoff_persisted`, and `handoff_error_code`. During `roots-request-failed` or `roots-empty`, a supplied `workspace_root` selector is ignored: the builder reads or writes no workspace/state data, emits `workspace_binding: not-established` and `workspace_root_used: false`, and makes no workspace-authority claim. Missing exact Root input, an incomplete lineage, Root/hash mismatch, semantic conflict, or—when Roots are available—a foreign, redirected, drifting, or ambiguous Root emits no artifact. Never claim production success from repository evidence.
