# Delivery Evidence output

Do not author `delivery-evidence` directly. Fresh Manual Review invokes the bundled deterministic local builder with exact Schema-6 Root and lineage, closed semantic input, and unprotected observations. The builder computes every ID and hash and rejects caller-provided verified claims, hashes, attestations, or receipts.

The builder returns paired Evidence and Review or neither. Local observations are limited to supported, partial, unavailable, or failed, so unprotected proof remains below verified. Finding-free supported required Checks may still establish achieved repository outcomes with no next action. Failed required Checks remain failed. Optional protected sealing may later append a new pair after validating protected attestations; it never edits the local pair.

Output leads with the human delivery decision, its concrete reason, required-Check outcome, scope impact, at most one proof boundary, and the Review artifact's exact next action. Full findings, Check grades, distinct limitations, paths, IDs, and Root, Evidence, Review, artifact, workspace, and snapshot hashes remain once in default-closed details. The two exact artifacts follow once each in default-closed disclosure without translation, quoting, or byte changes. Presentation excludes concrete execution data.
