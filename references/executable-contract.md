# Executable root plan

Required identity fields are `artifact: work-plan`, `schema: 3`, stable `id`, `status`, `intent_ready`, `decision_boundary: repository-delivery`, `runtime_relevant`, `risk`, `design_depth`, `automation_profile_max`, `writer_tier_required`, `assurance_score`, `assurance_profile`, `assurance_override`, nullable `assurance_override_decision_id`, and explicit `hard_triggers`. `created_at`, `source`, and non-authoritative `extensions` are optional. The validator recomputes and compares assurance and minimum writer tier but never fills missing semantic fields. Objectives come from plan content, not duplicated frontmatter.

Root construction starts only after the Planning skill's Intent Interview resolves every material human decision. Record answers as `DEC-*` sources; an allowed blocking prose fallback never accompanies a plan draft.

The plan semantically covers intent/decisions, Objectives, baseline evidence, scope, execution steps, verification, operational readiness, and risk/closeout. The parser tolerates aliases in received artifacts, but native generation uses the canonical H2 and table form in the Plan container contract. Readiness resolves Goal, Actor, Outcome, Non-goals, Constraints, Repository boundary, Acceptance evidence, Critical assumptions, Operational impact, Review risk, and material open decisions. A ready plan requires no remaining material open decision. Trivial lean work may omit DEC, incidental-scope, and explicit control boilerplate.

Each Objective maps to at least one outcome-oriented step, Completion Probe, and required Root Check. Every Check declares `machine-verifiable`, `human-review-required`, or `human-approval-required`; missing classification is invalid and never defaults to machine-verifiable. Required/permitted/prohibited scope remains explicit. Check definitions preserve an expected outcome; execution may use an equivalent in-scope method when it documents the actual method and rationale without weakening evidence. Security or hard-trigger substitutions require equally strong proof or `replan`.

Assurance factors remain Failure impact 0–3, Irreversibility 0–2, Uncertainty 0–2, Evidence weakness 0–2, and Change surface 0–1. Derived profiles are lean 0–3, standard 4–6, deep 7–10; any hard trigger forces deep. Lowering still requires an explicit human DEC and is impossible for a hard trigger. Expensive Pareto decisions are recorded only when included, deferred, or trigger-required.

Design depth is separate from assurance and automation eligibility. Follow [the design contract](design-contract.md). Auto-capable roots freeze complete, closed `automation_bounds`; absent or incomplete bounds are invalid. Manual roots omit `automation_bounds`. Full/deep/hard-trigger work requires `writer_tier_required: escalated`; other roots may explicitly raise the tier. Runtime-relevant work plans repository-verifiable signal, failure detection, and recovery. Native execution verifies the result and emits full initial evidence. Push, PR, merge, deploy, production access, and production-success claims remain outside the repository-delivery boundary.
