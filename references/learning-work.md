# Learning across the workflow

Keep learning in the native task's existing reports and handoffs. Reading this guidance, recording candidates, or offering learning does not commission it. Do not create a separate log, state engine, or required document format.

Capture reusable insights from the work, including successful corrections and diagnosis. For each candidate, explain the lesson, future benefit, supporting observations, scope and conditions, and proposed change with its destination. Use recognizable names, not formal IDs. Keep unconfirmed candidates distinct from review-confirmed lessons; a one-off observation is not a permanent rule.

Every subsequent phase report and handoff carries the complete, compact content of all open candidates, not just additions or a reference to earlier learnings. Include evidence references. Retain concise outcomes and reasons for integrated, merged, rejected, or superseded candidates, with their destinations or successors where relevant. Keep those records and their basis accessible throughout the workflow; supply text when the recipient cannot access references. Missing history or evidence stays an explicit gap, never a claim of a complete collection. Preserve unaffected work while resolving the gap before dependent learning.

Reconcile candidates whenever relevant work changes, and reassess both inherited and new candidates at every Review. Check the current repository, guidance, and later observations. Merge duplicates; distinguish conditions and scopes before treating lessons as contradictory. Refine, replace, or reject a candidate only with supporting evidence and a reason; newer does not automatically mean correct. Keep unresolved conflicts open and withhold the affected changes. Independent confirmed candidates may proceed. Recheck integrated lessons when later work affects their basis; propose a bounded update or removal for the next learning assignment rather than silently changing guidance during another phase.

At every Review, group the currently confirmed, applicable lessons into an optional learning offer with their benefits and the matching action. A completed Review may establish a lesson even when the overall goal still needs corrections; the lesson's own basis must be supported. Keep necessary corrections and missing acceptance proof prominent. Offers neither change the Review judgment nor add a required step to an achieved goal. With no eligible lessons, omit the offer. Unaccepted candidates remain open for later rounds; an explicit rejection is recorded with its reason.

On a commissioned learn-from-work invocation, consider all open candidates unless the human selects a narrower scope. Reconcile the collection again before edits, including interactions among the proposed changes and with existing guidance. Require completed Review evidence for each lesson and verify it still applies. Ask about unresolved consequential content, authority, or destination choices before the affected edits; continue independent authorized learning. Do not turn unsupported observations into rules or silently discard blocked candidates.

Use existing project guidance or appropriate skills and learning conventions. Save only bounded, reusable, demonstrated guidance; preserve unrelated instructions. Guidance updates must not implement product, tool, or verifier repairs. Route those and Workflow plugin changes beyond bounded guidance updates into concrete future development assignments with evidence, benefit, destination, expected outcome, and checks. Keep these pending until separately handled or explicitly rejected; learn-from-work does not authorize their implementation. Personal memory requires an explicit request for that memory.

After edits, read back the affected guidance and check that the combined result is consistent and preserves unrelated content. Only verified saved changes count as integrated. Report destinations, evidence, integrated or merged lessons, rejected or superseded lessons and reasons, and the complete remaining open collection. Partial write failures leave unfinished work open; report partial results accurately and repair only own bounded changes when authorized. On repeated invocations, compare actual saved guidance before adding anything, including after an interrupted prior invocation; do not duplicate rules or claim an unsaved change succeeded.
